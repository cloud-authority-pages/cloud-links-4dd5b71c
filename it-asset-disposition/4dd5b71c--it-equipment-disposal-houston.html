Our ITAD Process: From Data Destruction to Responsible Recycling

Laptop Assets technicians affix serialized tags and log each item into a secure transport manifest before the truck departs the facility. Upon arrival at the processing center, assets undergo an initial visual inspection to identify units suitable for refurbishment versus those slated for materials recovery. Data-bearing components receive NIST Special Publication 800-88 compliant sanitization using NIST-approved software or degaussing, depending on drive type and security tier. Secure Hard Drive Destruction and Component Refurbishment

IT equipment disposal houston must balance two competing priorities: eliminating data exposure while extracting maximum residual value.

Solid-state drives that once stored intellectual property or customer databases receive either cryptographic erasure followed by physical shredding or degaussing plus crushing, depending on the sensitivity classification. Witnessed destruction events are available for organizations that prefer visual confirmation before drives leave the facility. Meanwhile, memory modules, CPUs, and power supplies from lower-risk assets undergo rigorous testing and refurbishment. Functional components are matched with compatible chassis, receive updated firmware, and re-enter the market under fresh warranty. This dual-track model reduces the total cost of disposal for Houston clients because resale credits offset processing fees. Metals that cannot be reused move into documented downstream channels, ensuring no environmental shortcuts. Detailed reporting lists every drive's serial number, sanitization method, and final disposition, giving compliance officers an unbroken thread from rack to recycler.
